# Social Proof & Testimonials Policy (Fixed)

## Rules
- Never invent names, job titles, or quotes.
- Never claim “50,000+ sold”, “4.9★”, or “98% satisfied” without verifiable data.
- Prefer real customer photos and short unedited quotes once they exist.
- Until real reviews arrive, use the transparent “new brand” messaging.

## Reviews Page Intro
We only publish verified reviews from real orders.  
As customers share their experience we will publish them here, unedited (except for privacy).

## How to Collect Real Reviews
1. After delivery + 7–14 days, send a short follow-up email asking for feedback and optional photo.
2. Offer a small incentive only if desired (e.g. future accessory discount), never for a specific star rating.
3. Publish both positive and constructive feedback.
4. Link every published review to a verified order when possible.
