# What It Does Not Do (Critical Trust Section)

Use this block prominently on the homepage, product pages, and FAQ.

---

**What AxonRing Does Not Do**

- Make contactless payments at Visa, Mastercard, or Apple Pay terminals  
  (It has no secure payment chip. You can store a payment *link* that opens on another phone.)

- Track heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO2), sleep stages, stress, ECG, or blood pressure  
  (Those require batteries and medical-grade sensors — a different product category.)

- Contain a battery, screen, sensors, or GPS  
  (The chip is completely passive.)

- Act as a hardware security key (FIDO / Passkey)

Any passive NFC ring claiming otherwise is overpromising. Those features belong to battery-powered smart rings or dedicated payment devices.

---

**Short version (for product cards or footers):**  
No terminal payments · No health tracking · No battery · No screen
