## What It Does (Four Cards)

### 1. Digital Business Card
Tap any NFC phone. Your name, number, email and socials appear instantly.  
No app needed on their side.

### 2. Link Sharing
Store any URL — LinkedIn, portfolio, menu, payment link, website.  
One tap opens it on the other phone.

### 3. Phone Automations
Trigger iOS Shortcuts or Android routines.  
Silence your phone, start a timer, open an app, or run a smart-home scene.

### 4. Access & Scenes
Enroll the ring in compatible door / building systems (where the administrator allows it).  
Or use phone automations to control lights, locks, and routines.
