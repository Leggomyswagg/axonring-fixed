Perfect Fit, Guaranteed

We never ship a finished ring until you confirm your size from a real physical kit.

1 You order

Choose Executive or Prestige and complete checkout. One-time payment. No subscription.

2 Free sizing kit ships first

Within 1–2 business days we dispatch a physical plastic sizing kit + instructions. Typical delivery 5–10 business days.

3 You confirm your size

Try the gauges. Reply to the sizing email (or use the size portal) with your exact US size (6–13). We do not make the ring until you confirm.

4 Your ring is made & ships

After confirmation, production + shipping takes 7–14 business days. You receive tracking. Setup takes about 5 minutes.

International customers receive a US ↔ UK ↔ EU ↔ mm conversion chart with the kit.
One free size exchange is available if the final ring does not fit after you confirmed from the kit (contact us within 14 days of receiving the ring).
