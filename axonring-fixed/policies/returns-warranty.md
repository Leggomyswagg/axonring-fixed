# Returns, Refunds & Warranty Policy (Honest Version)

**Effective immediately for all AxonRing NFC orders.**

## 30-Day Returns
- Full refund, no questions asked, within 30 days of delivery.
- Ring must be returned in original condition.
- Customer pays return shipping unless the product is defective.
- Refund is processed to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after we receive the ring.
- Email support@axonring.com (or your real support address) to start a return. Include order number.

## Warranty
- **Executive:** 1 year from delivery date  
- **Prestige:** 2 years from delivery date  

**What is covered**
- Chip failure under normal use
- Material defects (cracking, coating failure under normal wear)

**What is not covered**
- Physical damage from impact, crushing, or modification
- Loss or theft
- Normal cosmetic wear
- Issues caused by third-party writing apps or incompatible systems

## Sizing
Because we ship a free physical sizing kit first, size-related returns are rare. If the final ring does not fit after you confirmed the size from the kit, contact us within 14 days of receiving the ring and we will work on a free size exchange (one time).

## Defective on Arrival
If the ring arrives damaged or the chip does not respond, contact us within 7 days. We will replace it at no cost and cover return shipping.
