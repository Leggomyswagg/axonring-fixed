## Key Terms to Publish

**Product description (legal-safe):**  
AxonRing is a passive near-field communication (NFC) ring containing an ISO 14443 compliant chip. It can store and share contact information, URLs, and simple automation payloads when tapped against a compatible NFC-enabled phone. It does not contain a battery, sensors, display, or secure payment element, and cannot perform contactless card payments at point-of-sale terminals or provide continuous health monitoring.

**Limitation of liability note (recommended):**  
AxonRing is not a medical device. It does not measure or claim to measure any biometric data. Access-control functionality depends on third-party systems and is not guaranteed for every building or lock.
