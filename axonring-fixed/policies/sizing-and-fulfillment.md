# Sizing Kit & Fulfillment Flow (Standardized)

## How It Works (Customer-Facing)

1. **You order**  
   Choose Executive or Prestige. Pay once. No subscription.

2. **Free sizing kit ships first**  
   Within 1–2 business days we dispatch a physical sizing kit (plastic gauges + instructions).  
   Delivery usually 5–10 business days depending on location.

3. **You confirm your size**  
   Try the gauges. Reply to the sizing email (or use the size portal) with your exact US size (6–13).  
   We do not make or ship the final ring until you confirm.

4. **Your ring is made and ships**  
   After confirmation, production + shipping takes 7–14 business days.  
   You receive tracking.

5. **Setup**  
   Takes about 5 minutes with a free NFC app. Guide included in the box.

## Internal Notes for Operations
- Never ship a finished ring without size confirmation.
- Keep sizing-kit inventory ready.
- Size confirmation email must be clear and one-click where possible.
- International customers: include conversion chart (US ↔ UK ↔ EU ↔ mm) in the kit and email.

## Messaging for Site & Emails
“We ship a free sizing kit first so your ring fits perfectly. No guessing. No returns because of size.”
