Frequently Asked Questions

Can it make contactless payments at terminals?

No. AxonRing has no secure payment chip, so it cannot pay at card terminals. You can store a payment link (PayPal.me, Venmo, Cash App) that opens on the other person’s phone when they tap your ring.

Does it track health metrics (heart rate, sleep, etc.)?

No. This is a passive NFC ring. Continuous health tracking requires batteries and sensors — a completely different product category. We do not claim those features.

Does it need charging?

Never. The chip is passive and draws power only when a phone reads it. There is no battery to die or replace.

How do I set it up?

About five minutes. Use a free NFC writing app (NFC Tools works on both iOS and Android). A step-by-step guide is included in every box. You can rewrite the ring anytime.

What about sizing?

We ship a free physical sizing kit first. Confirm your exact size, then the ring is made and shipped. US sizes 6–13.
