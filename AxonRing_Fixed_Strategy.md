# AxonRing — Fixed Product Strategy & Website Blueprint
**Date:** August 2026  
**Purpose:** Resolve credibility gaps, eliminate contradictory claims, and position the brand competitively against Oura, Ultrahuman, RingConn, and Samsung Galaxy Ring.

---

## 1. Core Problem Summary (What Was Broken)

| Issue | Current State (axonring.com) | Impact |
|-------|------------------------------|--------|
| Contradictory tech claims | “No Battery Required” + ECG, micro-display, continuous HR/SpO2, GPS, BP | Destroys trust; physics-impossible combination at claimed price/form factor |
| Overstated payments | “Tap to pay Visa/Mastercard/Apple Pay anywhere” | Secure element + tokenization required; simple NFC chips cannot do this |
| Mixed product identity | Passive NFC jewelry marketed as full smart ring | Confuses buyers and invites unfavorable comparisons |
| Inflated social proof | “50,000+ sold”, 4.9★, generic testimonials | No independent verification found |
| Vague specs | No clear sensor list, battery life, water rating, or app capabilities | Buyers cannot compare fairly |
| No honest limitations | Claims everything; never states what it cannot do | High return risk and reputation damage |

---

## 2. Fixed Product Strategy

### Two Clear Lines (Recommended)

**Line A — AxonRing NFC (Passive / No Battery)**  
Primary focus. Honest, differentiated, low-risk.  
- Sealed ceramic or titanium composite  
- ISO 14443 NTAG chip (rewritable)  
- Functions: Digital business card, any URL/link, phone automations (Shortcuts / Tasker), limited access-control enrollment where systems allow  
- Explicit non-claims: No terminal payments, no health sensors, no display, no battery  
- Price: $149–$349  
- Positioning: “The ring that never needs charging. Share, unlock, trigger — with one tap.”

**Line B — AxonRing Health (Active Smart Ring) — Optional Future**  
Only if real sensor hardware + validated app can be delivered.  
- PPG, temperature, accelerometer  
- 7–12 day battery, magnetic charging  
- Sleep stages, HRV, SpO2, activity, readiness score  
- Free app, Apple Health / Google Fit sync  
- No subscription  
- Price: $279–$399  
- Do **not** claim ECG, blood pressure, micro-display, or GPS until clinically/technically proven

**Immediate action:** Keep Line A as the core offering. Pause or heavily revise any “Ultra / Elite with display & ECG” claims until hardware exists.

## 3. Corrected Feature Set (What to Claim)

### AxonRing NFC — Honest Capabilities
**Out of the box**
- Digital business card (vCard) — name, phone, email, socials appear on any NFC phone
- Link sharing — any URL (portfolio, LinkedIn, payment link, menu, etc.)
- Phone automations — trigger iOS Shortcuts or Android routines by tapping own phone
- Fully rewritable with free apps (NFC Tools, etc.)

**With setup / existing systems**
- Building / door access (only if the access system supports and enrolls the chip)
- Smart-home scene triggers (via phone automation)

**Explicitly does NOT**
- Make contactless card payments at terminals
- Track heart rate, SpO2, sleep, stress, ECG, or blood pressure
- Have a battery, screen, or sensors
- Work as a hardware security key (FIDO)

### Materials & Practical Specs
- Hypoallergenic ceramic or titanium composite
- Water-resistant (showers, swimming, daily wear)
- Sizes US 6–13
- Free physical sizing kit shipped first
- 1–2 year warranty on chip/material failure
- 30-day no-questions returns
